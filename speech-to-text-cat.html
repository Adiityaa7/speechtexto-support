TalkPurr: Offline Voice Typing

Formerly SpeechtoTextCat. The app has a new name; its privacy practices remain the same.

Privacy policy and support for the Windows app.

Effective 12 October 2026 · Publisher: Adiityaaa7 (Aditya Patil)

Your voice stays on your PC

TalkPurr uses your microphone only while you are recording. Recordings are held in memory, processed locally using the bundled Cactus Whistle speech model, and discarded after processing or cancellation. The app does not upload recordings or transcripts, run analytics, serve ads, or require an account. It does not save audio files.

Text and preferences

The most recent transcript is kept in app memory so you can review, edit, or copy it. Clear removes it from the app, and quitting discards it. Closing the window leaves the app running in the system tray. Choose Quit from the tray menu to exit.

Shortcut, language, recording-mode preferences and custom vocabulary are stored locally in your Windows user profile under SpeechtoTextCat/settings.json in LocalAppData. Save preferences writes these settings to your PC. Uninstalling a portable copy may leave this settings folder; you can delete the folder to remove it.

Typing into other apps

The global shortcut captures the identity of the focused control through Windows accessibility APIs. The app compares this identity before inserting a transcript and checks for password and read-only fields. It does not read or retain existing document text, browser history, or passwords. It sends Unicode keystrokes only for the recognized transcript and does not automatically submit messages.

When you select Copy text, your transcript is written to the Windows clipboard. Windows clipboard history and cloud synchronization, if enabled on your PC, are governed by your Windows settings and Microsoft's policies. The app does not read your clipboard for automatic insertion.

Other services

Microsoft handles Store downloads, purchases if applicable, updates and Windows diagnostics under its own policies. Opening the model-credit link launches your browser; that website has its own privacy policy. If you email support, the publisher receives the message and attachments you choose to send. Please omit private audio and personal text unless necessary for your request.

Get started

Click an editable text field, hold Ctrl + Alt + V, wait for the Listening indicator, speak, and release to transcribe. Settings includes Ctrl + Shift + Space, F8, and a press-to-toggle mode. Fn combinations depend on keyboard firmware and are not supported as a universal shortcut. Recordings can last up to two minutes.

English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Dutch and Polish are supported. Hindi and Marathi are not supported by this model. Automatic typing works with many standard editable controls; elevated windows, remote sessions and custom controls may require manual copying. Review recognition results before relying on them.
