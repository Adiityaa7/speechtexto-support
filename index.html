THE FIRST TWO MINUTES

Make yourself at home.

01

Open speechTexto

Move your app into Applications, open that copy, and choose Quick setup.

02

Allow two permissions

Allow the microphone to record your voice. Enable Accessibility in System Settings so speechTexto can type into the focused editor.

03

Hold. Speak. Release.

Click an editable field, hold the Right ⌥ key, speak, then release. Your words are inserted after the recording finishes.

ONE SHORTCUT AWAY

YOUR VOICE

Choose your language.

English, Spanish, Italian, German, French, Dutch and Polish are supported. For Mexican Spanish, select Spanish. Hindi and Marathi are not supported. Recognition can vary by accent and background noise.

YOUR APPS

Check the editor.

Automatic typing has been tested in TextEdit. Support varies by editor; WhatsApp and Microsoft Teams have not yet been validated. Password fields are excluded. No messages are sent automatically.
