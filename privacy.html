PRIVACY POLICY · OCTOBER 6, 2026

Your words.
Your Mac.

This policy covers the speechTexto direct-download app and this support website, operated by Aditya Patil.

Your voice stays on your Mac

speechTexto processes recordings locally using its bundled speech model. The app does not upload recordings or transcripts, use advertising or analytics SDKs, track you, or require an account. No internet connection is needed to dictate.

Microphone access

Recording starts only when you use a dictation control or shortcut. You can stop or discard a recording, and each recording is limited to 30 seconds. Microphone access can be revoked in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone.

App access

The direct-download edition uses Accessibility access to identify the focused editor, read its text and selection to guard against inserting into the wrong place, observe the dictation shortcut, and insert your transcript. macOS grants broad app-control access; speechTexto uses it for these dictation features. It does not request Full Disk Access or Screen Recording. Accessibility access can be revoked in System Settings.

Local storage and deletion

Audio buffers are held in memory during recording and transcription, then released. Your latest transcript remains in memory until you clear it or quit. Temporary editor text used to verify insertion can remain in memory until another recording or the app exits. Released memory is not securely erased. Language, shortcut, insertion method, personal vocabulary and setup preferences are saved locally. Erase personal vocabulary in Settings and use the trash button to clear your transcript.

Clipboard and other apps

Type at cursor does not change your clipboard. Copy places the transcript on the system clipboard. Optional Paste compatibility mode temporarily uses the clipboard and attempts to restore its prior contents after two seconds, unless another app changed it. Clipboard managers, Universal Clipboard and destination apps may retain or transmit text under their own settings and policies. Clearing a transcript in speechTexto does not remove copies from other apps or your clipboard. speechTexto never submits a message or presses Return for you.

Typing test

Test typing opens a temporary plain-text document in TextEdit and inserts a fixed sample. It does not record audio. TextEdit may save the test document according to its own behavior; you can close or delete that document yourself.

Your choices

You can use Preview only, decline or revoke app-control access, turn off Open at login, clear transcripts and vocabulary, and quit speechTexto. Opening an external support or privacy website uses your browser and that website's privacy practices. This explanation covers the app itself; macOS diagnostics and any information you choose to send for support are handled separately.

This support website

This site uses no analytics scripts, advertising, contact forms, or cookies added by speechTexto. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process connection and usage information, including your IP address, under its privacy statement.

Email support

If you email adityapatil206999@gmail.com, your email address, message and any attachments are received through Gmail and used to answer your request. This is separate from the app, which does not send support data automatically. Send only the information needed to diagnose the issue. You can request deletion of your support correspondence at the same address; copies may remain in service-provider backups or where retention is required.

Changes and contact

We will update this page if the app’s data practices change. The date above identifies the current policy. Contact Aditya Patil at adityapatil206999@gmail.com with privacy questions.
